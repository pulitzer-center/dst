# Intro 

The purpose of this repo is to simply display the impact tracking I am taking from my digital security trainings. 

## Process

I reach out to colleagues that have a greater reach to external fellows, cohorts and journalists at a global scale and ask if 
they would be interested in having me lead a digital security training. If they report interest, we set up a time for me to 
attend a meeting/webinar/event. 

After the training is held I reach out to the team lead and provide them with a link to my feedback questionaire. I allow 1 weekof time for members to fill out the form. In that time I take the attendee's email and ensure that I reach out the following 
week in hopes to gain their feedback.

Once feedback is received, I update my html code and publish to my git site.
