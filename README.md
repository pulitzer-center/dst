# Digital Security Training Initiative
## Strategic Integration & Impact Tracking

### About This Initiative

As the IT Manager at the Pulitzer Center, I've developed a strategic approach to integrate digital security training into our organizational workflow. This repository documents our methodology for embedding security awareness across all teams while continuously improving our training effectiveness through data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Vision

### Our Goal
Transform digital security from a standalone IT function into an integrated component of every team's workflow, ensuring that security training reaches journalists, fellows, and cohorts through their existing program touchpoints.

### The Opportunity
By partnering directly with teams across the organization, we can:
- **Scale** security training to reach more journalists globally
- **Customize** content to specific program needs
- **Measure** impact through systematic feedback
- **Iterate** based on real-world data

---

## 📋 The Process: From Outreach to Impact

### Phase 1: Team Engagement & Pulse Check

**Initial Outreach**
- Schedule meetings with each interested team/department
- Understand their programs and external stakeholders
- Identify security concerns specific to their work

**Key Questions We Explore:**
- What journalists/fellows/cohorts does your team work with?
- What regions or topics do they cover?
- Have they expressed security concerns?
- Would security training add value to your programs?
- How can we integrate training into existing workflows?

**Outcome:** Collaborative decision on whether and how to incorporate security training

### Phase 2: Customized Planning

**Timeline:** Within 2-3 weeks of initial meeting

**Drill-Down Process:**
- Identify specific threat models for their journalists
- Select relevant training modules
- Customize examples to their context
- Schedule training sessions
- Coordinate logistics and materials

**Deliverables:**
- Tailored training agenda through pre-training assesment 
- Resource package specific to their needs

### Phase 3: Training Delivery

**Approach:**
- Interactive, scenario-based sessions
- Hands-on practice with tools (optional)
- Q&A tailored to their specific contexts
- Follow-up resources and support channels (email it@pulitzercenter.org)

**Format Options:**
- Virtual workshops for global cohorts
- In-person intensive sessions
- Pre-recorded sessions

### Phase 4: Feedback Collection

**Immediate Post-Training:**
- Share standardized feedback form
- Capture quantitative metrics
- Collect qualitative insights
- Document specific use cases

**Feedback Areas:**
- Training effectiveness (star scale)
- Relevance to their work
- Likelihood to implement learnings
- Additional topics of interest
- Suggestions for improvement

### Phase 5: Impact Tracking & Visualization

**Data Processing:**
- Responses compiled and analyzed
- Key metrics extracted
- Trends identified across programs
- Success stories documented

**Public Dashboard:**
- Results displayed on our GitHub Pages site
- Real-time impact visualization
- Transparent accountability
- Evidence-based improvement tracking
